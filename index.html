Born around a fire pit

Angithi started in 2012 as a small family kitchen with one clay tandoor. Chef Rohan Mehra learned his grandmother’s recipes and kept her one rule: no shortcuts, only slow heat.

Today we grind our own spices, marinate for 24 hours and cook everything over charcoal or wood. Our menu changes with the seasons, but the fire never goes out.
